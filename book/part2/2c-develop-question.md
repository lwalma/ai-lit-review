## Recap Standard Practice: Defining Components of Your Research Question and Specifying Further

Formulating a research question means defining specifically the who, what, where and why you want to find information about. You combine these elements into a question with "how". After formulating an initial question, you further can make the who, what, where and why more broader or more narrow, partly based on the orientation and literature exploration you have done.

## AI Assist: Revising Your Draft Research Question

Once you have formulated a research question, you can use general tools to reflect on it and improve the formulation. It it not advised to ask AI tools for suggestions for research questions. AI tools have no understanding of what is relevant or interesting to pursue. Moreover, it is worthwile to use your own creativity to come up with an original research question that is worth investigating. General AI tools can be used as an assistant to further specify your inital research question. Ideally, you want to have a specific basis to start from: the stronger your input, the stronger the output will be.

```{admonition} Example Prompt
:class: dropdown note

You are an expert in [research field]. I am working on a literature review paper on [topic]. I have developed the following research question: "[draft research question]". Give me suggestions to improve this research question. Present the suggestions in a bullet list. For each suggestion, explain how the research question can be adjusted and how that improves it.
```

If you are looking for specific improvements, you can make your prompt more specific. For example, if you already know that your research question is too broad, you ask for suggestions to make it more specific.

```{admonition} Example Prompt
:class: dropdown note

You are an expert in [research field]. I am working on a literature review paper on [topic]. I have developed the following research question: "[draft research question]". Give me suggestions to make this research question more specific. Present the suggestions in a bullet list.
```

```{admonition} Guided Activity: Develop a Research Question
:class: dropdown tip

**Step 1: Develop your initial research question (without AI!)**
Have a look at your exploration of academic literature. Identify the who, what, where and why of what you want to study.

**Step 2: Use general AI tools to refine your draft research question**

Select a general AI tool
Options
- Copilot
- ChatGPT
- Claude

Example Prompt:

_I am a [x year] student in [study]. I am working on a literature review paper on [topic]. I have developed the following research question: [draft research question]. Give me suggestions to improve the research questions. Present the suggestions in a bullet list. For each suggestion, explain how the research question can be adjusted and how that improves it._

**Step 3: Reflect**
1. How did the AI tool’s version of your research question differ from your own draft?  
2. Which of its suggestions made your question clearer, narrower, or more focused? 
3. Which suggestions will you include?
```
